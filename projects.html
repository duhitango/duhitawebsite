Our Projects

At Duhita, we undertake various projects to empower women and children in North Bengal. Here are some of our key initiatives:

Education for All

Providing educational support and resources to children in tea gardens and underserved communities. We run after-school programs and distribute school supplies.

Women's Skill Development

Offering vocational training in sewing, handicrafts, and digital literacy to help women become financially independent.

Healthcare Outreach

Organizing health camps and awareness programs to improve access to medical care and promote healthy living in remote areas.

Community Empowerment

Building community centers and support networks to foster social change and provide a safe space for women and children.
